# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

FaithFull Scholars contributors, maintainers and community members are expected to communicate with integrity, professionalism and mutual respect.

This project serves scholars and institutions across many theological traditions. Contributors bring their own convictions, and that is welcome. In this project's spaces, we treat every tradition represented on the platform with charity and fairness.

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and confessional or denominational traditions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the wider community and for the scholars and institutions the platform serves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal, political or sectarian attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission. This includes religious affiliation, doctrinal statements, or physical or email addresses.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers privately. Every complaint will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
