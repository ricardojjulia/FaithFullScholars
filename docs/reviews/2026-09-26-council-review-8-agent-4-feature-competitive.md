## 2. Competitive Landscape Differentiation

Against general platforms (LinkedIn, Academia.edu, HigherEdJobs) and static ecclesiastical boards (ETS / SBL job listings):
- **LinkedIn / HigherEdJobs**: Lack granular doctrinal indexing, confessional lens matrices, dual-protection revision staging (ADR 0005), and board search dockets.
- **Academia.edu / ResearchGate**: Focus on pre-print PDF sharing, with zero hiring workflow, institutional inquiry gating, or contract management.
- **ETS / SBL Job Boards**: Passive, unformatted text listings lacking interactive candidate dossiers, AI matching, or multi-institution consortium accounts.

FaithFull Scholars owns the end-to-end theological faculty lifecycle: from recruitment and vetting to course licensing, sabbatical swaps, and keynote speaking.
