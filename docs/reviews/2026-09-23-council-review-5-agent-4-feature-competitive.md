### 1. Theological Higher Education Domain Fit
- **Multi-Campus Systems**: Denominational seminaries routinely maintain multi-campus networks sharing provosts, boards, and accreditation (e.g. RTS 6-campus system). Without consortium architecture, sister campuses face duplicate administrative burdens and siloed candidate data.
- **Consortia Partnerships**: Regional alliances (ARTS, BTI, ACTS) rely on cross-registration and shared faculty. FaithFull Scholars provides the first specialized platform uniting confessional alignment with federated faculty collaboration.
- **Economic Value**: Empowers deans to pool recruiting investments, share visiting doctoral faculty for modular intensives, and lower search costs.
